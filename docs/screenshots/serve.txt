Notes on the shared block
-------------------------

* `--attach 127.0.0.1:5002` is SCHEMELESS and was normalised to
  `http://127.0.0.1:5002`. Attach mode never restarts or stops the
  upstream; it only points stone-llama's OpenAI-compatible endpoint at it.
* `/healthz` is the daemon's own health route and carries
  `X-Stone-Llama: 1`. The literal `/health` path is an upstream
  passthrough (Server: uvicorn) and carries NO marker header — see §2.
* `ps` and `stop` are shown exactly as OBSERVED at this revision
  (4f020dc). They are not a statement of final semantics: at this
  revision `ps` is not read-only (with no daemon it auto-starts a
  detached one, and a failed /healthz probe can remove `daemon.json`).
  That behaviour was reported by the independent verification run and is
  under active fix — treat the block above as observed-at-revision.
